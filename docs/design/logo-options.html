equiCast — brand mark

Icon, wordmark, and tagline

Finalised Candlestick Spear + “Cast your equity forward.”

The mark is Candlestick Spear — three real OHLC candlesticks (body + wick) ascending, the tallest candle's upper wick sharpened into a spearpoint breaking past the frame. The tagline is “Cast your equity forward.” Colors are Palette A — Violet & Plum — see palette-options.html for the full color rationale. The alternative icon directions and taglines considered are recapped further down. Colors below are hard-coded hex (#4B65D9 etc.), exact OKLCH→sRGB conversions of the token values, not eyeballed — the SVG exports can't rely on oklch() or CSS variables the way the live app can.

Alternative icon directions

The six other icon directions explored across three rounds, kept here for reference. Candlestick Spear was chosen for being the most literal "trading" glyph of the set — real OHLC candles, not an invented metaphor — while still landing the "cast forward" idea in the spearpoint.

Bars & Pingalternative
Round 1 & 2 Literal bar chart with a broadcast ping — read too generically "analytics dashboard," not trading-specific.
Trend & Signalalternative
Round 2 Ascending line with radiating arcs — fluid, but the arcs read closer to a wifi/broadcast icon than trading.
Orbit & Satellitealternative
Round 2 Abstract open ring — most minimal of the set, but too abstract; no trading or "cast" cue on its own.
Price Boltalternative
Round 3 Diagonal lightning bolt — graphically punchy, but read as generic "fast/power" rather than trading.
Target Dartalternative
Round 3 Bullseye pierced by a dart — strong "hit your target" idea, but the most visually busy of the four.
Breakout Flagalternative
Round 3 Real flag/pennant continuation pattern — a nice insider nod, but less legible at favicon scale.
