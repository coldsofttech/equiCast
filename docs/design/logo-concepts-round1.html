equiCast — brand mark

Icon, wordmark, and tagline candidates

Built on Palette A (Resource Planner's violet). The mark is a small ascending bar-chart glyph with a broadcast "ping" off the tallest bar — equity trending up, cast forward. Colors are hard-coded hex (#4B65D9 etc.), exact OKLCH→sRGB conversions of the token values, not eyeballed — the SVG exports can't rely on oklch() or CSS variables the way the live app can.

Tagline shortlist

Six candidates spanning three tones. Pick one (or none — the lockup works without it), and I'll fold it into a tagline lockup variant.

Functional Track your portfolio. Forecast what's next. States the two core jobs plainly — tracking and forecasting.
Functional Clear tracking. Confident forecasts. Same territory, tighter two-beat rhythm.
Wordplay Cast your equity forward. Leans on the name itself — "equity" and "cast/forecast" in one line, ownable.
Wordplay Your equity, forecasted. Tighter still; plays on "your future, forecasted" but grounded in equity.
Punchy Invest with foresight. Short, confident, no explicit pun — reads well at favicon-adjacent sizes too.
Punchy See further. Grow smarter. Most aspirational of the six — least tied to the literal feature set.
